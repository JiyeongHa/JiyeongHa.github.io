About Me

I'm a PhD candidate at NYU with 8 years of experience in computational modeling and data analysis of human visual systems. My research involves processing complex 3D anatomical structures from neuroimaging data (including diffusion MRI and 7T functional MRI), requiring expertise in mesh processing, machine learning, statistical modeling, and Python automation.

I specialize in designing quantitative studies to map human biological systems, translating multidimensional datasets into actionable insights. I love turning complex neural data into something interpretable — uncovering structure in noise, building tools for reproducible research, and connecting human perception to technology.

In my free time, I spend time with my cat Shasha (my first foster and the only foster failure). I also foster kittens and cats in need — please reach out if you're interested in fostering!

Research Experience

Co-Lead Researcher: Large-Scale 3D Anatomical Mapping Sep 2023 – Present
New York University
  • Performed anatomical mapping and analysis of 3D brain structures (hV4, VO1, VO2) across 362 hemispheres from 181 subjects, creating the largest public dataset of ventral visual areas
  • Verified custom annotation software for 3D anatomical boundaries on cortical surfaces by testing inter-subject and inter-rater reliabilities
  • Performed heritability analysis on 84 twin pairs (MZ/DZ) using ICC and Falconer's index to assess genetic vs. environmental influences on anatomical structure
  • Processed multi-terabyte 7T MRI datasets using automated pipelines for cortical surface reconstruction and alignment (FreeSurfer)
  • Conducted inter-rater reliability analysis using Linear Mixed Effects models to validate boundary consistency across 5 raters
  • Paper in preparation for Journal of Neuroscience; data and code to be released on OSF and GitHub
Visiting Scholar: 3D White Matter Tractography Analysis Sep 2022 – Dec 2022
Stanford University
  • Led a multi-institutional collaboration to identify spatial patterns of white matter tracts that predict transitions between retinotopic and category-selective visual areas, producing a scientific presentation at OHBM 2023
  • Processed 3D white matter tract terminations from diffusion MRI using Automated Fiber Quantification (pyAFQ) pipeline
  • Analyzed endpoint density distributions across cortical surfaces to identify anatomical boundaries between brain regions
  • Integrated multi-modal neuroimaging data (diffusion MRI and functional MRI) to characterize structure-function relationships
Graduate Researcher: Computational Modeling of Human Visual Cortex Sep 2021 – Present
New York University
  • Built computational models in PyTorch on large-scale fMRI datasets that predict visual cortex responses based on spatial frequency information, resulting in a first-author paper
  • Extended the model into image-computable vision framework by incorporating multi-scale, multi-orientation image decomposition (steerable pyramid), resulting in a first-author paper (in prep.)
  • Conducted statistical modeling (bootstrap, cross-validation for model comparison) and simulations to evaluate model reliability
  • Developed scalable data pipelines (Snakemake + Slurm HPC) for end-to-end analysis of multi-terabyte datasets, ensuring reproducibility and efficiency
  • Released open-source code/data on GitHub and OSF
  • Recognized with the $5K NYU Shakrani Summer Research Fellowship and the Elsevier/Vision Research Student Award
  • Communicated findings in 4 talks and 2 posters including scientific conferences (VSS 2023, OHBM 2024)
Research Staff: Audio-visual Integration & Color Perception Mar 2018 – Aug 2021
Center for Neuroscience Imaging Research, South Korea
  • Led multisensory integration research on how audiovisual inputs (melody and visual motion) are integrated at brain/behavior levels, using fMRI and psychophysics, resulting in 2+ conference presentations and a presenter award
