About Me

I’m interested in how we perceive the world. Imagine the world as a grid—each patch is seen differently based on its location, content (i.e., color and orientations), and cognition (i.e., attention, memory). I identify these factors through experiments and build data-driven models to predict perception.

My research interest lies at the intersection of vision science, machine learning, and data analysis. I love turning complex neural data into something interpretable — uncovering structure in noise, building tools for reproducible research, and connecting human perception to technology.

In my free time, I spend time with my cat Shasha (my first foster and the only foster failure). I also foster kittens and cats in need— please reach out if you're interested in fostering!
