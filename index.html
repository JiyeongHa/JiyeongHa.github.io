About Me

Hi, I’m Jiyeong Ha (also known as "Haji"). I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Perception & Cognition at New York University, where I study computational modeling of visual perception.

I’m interested in how we see the world. Imagine the world as a grid— each patch is perceived differently based on its location, content (i.e. color and orientations), and information from other modalities. I identify these factors through experiments and build data-driven models to predict perception.

My work lives at the intersection of vision science, machine learning, and data analysis. I love turning complex neural data into something interpretable — uncovering structure in noise, building tools for reproducible research, and connecting human perception to technology.
