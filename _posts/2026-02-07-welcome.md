Welcome! I'm excited to start this blog as a place to share my thoughts and experiences in vision science, computational neuroscience, and navigating the PhD journey.

## What to expect

I plan to write about:

- **Research insights** -- interesting findings from my work on spatial frequency maps, 3D brain anatomy, and computational modeling of visual perception
- **Technical tutorials** -- practical guides on neuroimaging analysis, Python workflows, and reproducible research
- **PhD life** -- the ups and downs of graduate school, tips for productivity, and lessons learned along the way

## Why a blog?

Academic papers are great for communicating results, but they don't capture the full picture. The dead ends, the debugging sessions, the "aha" moments -- those are the stories that make research human. I hope this blog can fill that gap.

I also believe that science communication is important. Not everyone reads journal papers, but many people are curious about how the brain works. This blog is my attempt to make my research more accessible.

## Stay tuned

I'll be posting regularly, so check back soon. If there's a topic you'd like me to write about, feel free to [reach out](mailto:jiyeong.ha@nyu.edu).
